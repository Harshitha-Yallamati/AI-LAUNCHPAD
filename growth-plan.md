# Growth plan

Target audience: final-year engineering students in India who want a practical first AI project. Value proposition: a free, beginner-friendly 60-minute guided online workshop.

| Channel | Goal | Tactic |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| College Communities | 250 | Department groups and campus clubs |
| Student Ambassadors | 175 | Unique first-touch referral links |
| Direct Outreach | 75 | Relevant opt-in communities and organic posts |

Seven-day plan: prepare creative and ambassador kit on day 1; launch communities on days 2–3; inspect attribution and enable strong ambassadors on days 4–5; concentrate remaining budget on the most efficient channel on day 6; run a final organic/referral push on day 7.

Budget: ₹1,000 ambassador rewards, ₹300 creative assets, ₹700 contingency. Track unique registrations, channel target completion, referral quality, duplicates, actual spend, and cost per unique registration. Assumptions: campaign execution is simulated; no students are contacted. Risks include invalid referrals, low community reach, and duplicate submissions.
